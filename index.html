chapter zero

I never was good at saying the big things out loud. But I have always been good at noticing — the way you push your glasses up with your ring finger, the exact moment your face changes when you're pretending not to laugh, how your hand finds mine like it was always supposed to be there.

So I built you something instead. Eight chapters. The café, the card, the watches, the photograph you didn't know I took. The ones I think about more than you'd ever guess.

Red roses, a string of tiny lights, and a card I cut and stuck and stayed up far too late on — because I wanted the birthday to feel like it was given, not just passed. The heart-shaped cutout is still my favourite part.

Two-tone gold and steel on your wrist, all silver on mine. Yours is louder than mine. Mine is twice the size. Neither of us will ever wear anything else — and you were the one who picked them out, which matters more than the watches.

Chapter Seven

Chapter Eight

The flowers, and the food, and you

You always order the one with the sauce. I always steal the bread. The flowers were never on the table for any reason — they were just there, like you, quietly making the whole thing better than it had any right to be.

one more thing

If I could press rewind,
I'd do it all again.

Every version of my life before you was fine. Perfectly fine. And that is exactly what makes you so impossible — you didn't just improve the story, you changed the whole genre. You made the ordinary parts worth keeping: a table, a plate of food, a mirror selfie at the wrong angle.

So here it is: the small museum of us. Eight chapters — and the next one gets written the moment it happens. Add to it whenever you want. It's yours.

P.S. — I'll never stop choosing you. Even on the boring days. Especially on those.
